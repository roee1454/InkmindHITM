# Ink Mind Tattoo & Piercing — Customer Conversation Reference

Real WhatsApp booking conversations between the studio (Shoham) and customers, cleaned up
and structured for use as reference / evaluation material for the customer-facing agent.

- **Source:** 10 exported WhatsApp `.txt` chats (`שיחה לקוח1–10.txt`). Files 2 and 10 are
  byte-identical, so there are **9 unique conversations** below.
- **Language:** Hebrew, verbatim. Spelling mistakes, typos and auto-corrupted words
  (`צריל`, `אוצה`, `לישות`, `מתאיםנ`, `הירב חג`, `דיר`, `09/1001/25` …) are **left exactly as
  written** — they are the messy real-world input the agent has to understand.
- **Speakers:** `Customer` = the phone number, `Studio` = `Ink Mind Tattoo & Piercing`
  (a human staff member — mostly דולב / דור answering, not a bot).
- **Quoted replies:** when a message begins by repeating the other party's previous message
  (a WhatsApp "reply"), the quoted part is shown as `>` and the real reply follows it.
- **Recurring template messages** (opening auto-reply, deposit request, booking confirmation,
  reminder, review request) are referenced as **→ Template A–F** and printed once in the
  [Appendix](#appendix--recurring-template-messages).
- `[image]` / `[link]` mark non-text messages.

## Notes for the agent (patterns visible across these 9 chats)

- **Two appointment types.** The studio distinguishes a **פגישת סקיצה** (sketch meeting, ~100–200₪
  paid at the meeting, doubles as the deposit) from the **תור לקעקוע** (the tattoo session itself).
  Many customers do the sketch meeting first, then book the tattoo afterwards.
- **The studio will not price or size an appointment without a rough direction** — a reference
  image or at least size + placement + style. It repeatedly pushes for this before booking
  (Conv 2 is the clearest example).
- **Deposit flow:** 100₪ / 200₪ via Bit or PayBox to `0528114746`, then a screenshot + a
  separate message with full name and phone. Deposit can be **waived for trusted returning
  clients** (Conv 3).
- **Artist availability is dominated by reserve duty (מילואים).** "ארשום אותך לרשימה של דור
  ונתעדכן" (I'll add you to דור's list and update you) is a recurring holding pattern, and
  several appointments get rescheduled by the studio.
- **דור** is the main artist referenced; **גאיה** and **דולב** also appear (דולב handles
  piercing).
- **Customers frequently quote the previous message** and answer underneath it, send several
  short messages in a row, use heavy slang and typos, and send bare links/images as "references".
- **Age:** at least one customer (Conv 4) is a minor (17) and it is not flagged or handled
  specially in the chat.
- **Same channel is used for after-care** (Conv 2 ink-on-wrap question, Conv 7 piercing) — not
  just booking.
- **Timezone / dates:** all times are Israel local, `DD/MM` or `DD.MM`. Note the real typo
  `09/1001/25` in Conv 5 and the Passover date confusion in Conv 9.
